# Combine live observations with explicitly labelled simulation

The portfolio uses OpenSky aircraft observations for current Saudi/UAE airspace and a synthetic operational history for analytics and model training. Official schedules and movement outcomes require airline or commercial-provider access, so the two evidence classes remain visibly labelled and are never merged into a claim of real airline performance.

## Consequences

The product can demonstrate streaming, analytics, and ML now, but every interface must preserve provenance and must describe model output as a scenario rather than an operational forecast.
